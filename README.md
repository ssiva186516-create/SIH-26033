## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
A digital platform connects farmers directly with consumers using harvest posters, UPI payments, and village pickup hubs.

It reduces middlemen, increases farmer income, lowers consumer prices, and reduces food wastage through pre-booking and bulk delivery.

It combines QR traceability, automated payments, village-level collection, and apartment bulk delivery using simple smartphones and existing technol

## Technical Approach
Payments & Logistics: Razorpay Route handles instant, automated multi-party UPI split payouts, while the Google Maps Distance Matrix API plans single-trip village pickup routes to residential drop-off hubs.

## Feasibility and Viability
* Technical Feasibility: The platform relies on proven, lightweight cloud APIs, standard Android smartphones, and India's existing UPI infrastructure—requiring zero expensive IoT sensors or proprietary hardware at the farm level.
 
* Operational Feasibility: Each village club nominates one tech-literate youth or Village Coordinator (similar to existing Self-Help Group or FPO models) to verify produce quality at a single village collection point, simplifying pickup logistics.
  
* Economic Viability: Instead of charging farmers high commissions, the platform sustains itself through a nominal 2–3% platform convenience fee paid by urban buyers/apartments and optional premium analytics for institutional buyers (restaurants, hostels, and supermarkets).
  
* Challenges & Mitigation:
	• Digital literacy barriers: Solved via voice-assisted regional UI and the Village Coordinator model.
	• High last-mile delivery costs: Solved by aggregating orders into apartment-level bulk drop-offs ("milk-run" delivery) rather than individual doorstep trips.


## Impact and Benefits
* Higher Farmer Income (+30% to 50%): Eliminating 3–4 layers of middlemen allows farmers to capture the true market value of their harvest with instant, transparent UPI payouts.
* Lower Consumer Prices (-15% to 25%): Urban households and apartment communities buy farm-fresh seasonal vegetables below supermarket retail rates.
* Reduced Post-Harvest Wastage: Seasonal dynamic pricing and 7-day harvest pre-booking align supply with real demand, preventing distress dumping during peak seasons.
* Farm-to-Fork Traceability: Every vegetable crate includes a village QR tag showing the exact village club, farmer group, and harvest timestamp, building consumer trust in local produce.
